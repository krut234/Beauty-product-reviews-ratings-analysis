📊 Beauty Product Reviews & Ratings Dashboard

📌 Project Overview

The Beauty Product Reviews & Ratings Dashboard is an interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze customer reviews, ratings, product prices, categories, and sentiment. The dashboard provides business insights into customer preferences and product performance.

🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall product review performance.
- Compare customer ratings across different products.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Analyze positive, neutral, and negative customer sentiment.
- Understand the relationship between product price and customer rating.
- Present insights through interactive visualizations.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Microsoft Excel
- Power Query
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)

📈 Dashboard Features

- Total Reviews
- Average Rating
- Average Product Price
- Product-wise Rating Analysis
- Category-wise Analysis
- Sentiment Analysis
- Price vs. Rating Analysis
- Interactive Product & Category Filters
- KPI Cards
- Charts & Visualizations

📊 Key Insights

- Identified products with higher customer ratings.
- Compared customer sentiment across different product categories.
- Analyzed the distribution of positive, neutral, and negative reviews.
- Examined the relationship between product price and customer ratings.
- Identified customer preferences based on review and rating patterns.
- Created an interactive dashboard to support business decision-making.
